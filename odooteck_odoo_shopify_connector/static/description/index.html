Key Features & Capabilities

🏬 Multi-Store Shopify Integration

Connect and operate multiple Shopify stores from a single Odoo 19 instance with store-specific warehouses, pricelists, currencies, companies, and sales teams.

🛡️ Lossless Staging Feeds Queue

Incoming product, variant, order, customer, and collection payloads land in staging queues with full raw JSON inspection, error diagnostics, and one-click re-processing.

🔄 Bi-directional Product & Variant Sync

Export products from Odoo to Shopify, or import Shopify catalogs into Odoo with automatic SKU, barcode, title, description, and multi-variant matrix cross-referencing.

📍 Multi-Location Stock Sync (Import & Export)

Pair individual Shopify fulfillment locations with Odoo warehouses and stock locations. Push on-hand quantities, import Shopify stock counts, and route order deliveries automatically.

📦 Automated Delivery Fulfillment Tracking

Validating a delivery order in Odoo immediately pushes tracking reference numbers and carrier names to Shopify, fulfilling orders and notifying customers automatically.

🏷️ Declarative Metafields Mapping Engine

Map custom Shopify metafields (materials, care guides, SEO titles, JSON data) directly to native or custom Odoo fields with zero coding and bi-directional direction control.

💳 Automated Refunds & Credit Notes

When orders are refunded in Shopify, the connector captures refund events, generates matching Customer Credit Notes in Odoo, and provides optional auto-posting.

⚡ Real-Time HMAC Webhooks & Audit Logs

Secure webhook ingestion with cryptographic HMAC-SHA256 signature verification, scheduled cron automation, and complete sync history logging.

How the Workflow Works

Hover or Click Image to Replay Animation ↻

Shopify events enter a staging feed for review and processing into Odoo records, ensuring zero data loss and reliable synchronization.

Shopify to Odoo 19 Data Pipeline
Click / Hover to Replay ↻
Step 1: Event Ingestion
Webhooks, Cron or Manual Sync

Shopify triggers an HMAC-verified webhook or Odoo fetches records via scheduled cron or the manual Sync Wizard.

Step 2: Staging Queue
Safe Raw Payload Buffer

Incoming payloads are staged in shopify.feed. If tax or mapping rules are missing, inspect raw JSON and re-process with one click.

Step 3: Core Odoo Processing
Orders, Stock & Invoices

Feeds convert into Sales Orders, stock moves in mapped warehouses, invoices generate, and tracking numbers push back to Shopify.
