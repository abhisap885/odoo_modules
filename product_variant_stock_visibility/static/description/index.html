Product Variant Stock Visibility FAQs

How do I enable this?

Open Website Settings and enable Grey Out Out-of-Stock Variant Options for the website.

What do shoppers see?

Unavailable attribute values are greyed out, struck through and disabled on the product page.

When are options recalculated?

The module checks availability when the product loads and whenever a shopper changes a variant option.

Which products are checked?

The stock check applies to storable products with variants on the Odoo Website Shop.

Does it respect Odoo stock rules?

Yes. A variant remains available when Odoo allows selling out of stock or its free quantity is above zero.

Does it support multiple websites?

Yes. The setting is stored on the active website, so each website can control the behavior independently.
